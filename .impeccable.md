## Design Context

### Users
People using a photo feed where an Orb-verified World ID ultimately anchors each pseudonymous human profile. During the ETHTokyo demo, a staging Human identity lets the team show the complete experience on real iPhones without an Orb scan. Guests can browse and inspect proof details before creating a profile.

### Brand Personality
Human, precise, and playful. A social photo app first, with verification visible when a viewer asks for it. The app should feel native to iPhone and trustworthy without reading like a developer test harness.

### Aesthetic Direction
Monochrome Pupille identity, the existing SVG eye mark as an in-app accent and PNG logo as the Home Screen icon. Native SwiftUI Liquid Glass, generous rounding, full photos, and a compact camera inspired by iPhone Camera. Keep verification green as a semantic success color; avoid unrelated accent colors. Follow the system appearance and support Dark Mode.

### Design Principles
- Put the photo and the person first; keep proof details one tap away.
- Center and simplify the World ID entry flow; reveal the handle field only when needed.
- Use glass for actionable surfaces and controls, leaving image content unobscured.
- Give actions immediate, restrained feedback and honor Reduce Motion.
- Preserve native iPhone touch targets, Dynamic Type, and useful guest access.
